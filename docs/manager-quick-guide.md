# Reliable Storage – Manager Quick Guide

**Who this is for:** Reliable Storage managers
**Reading time:** ~3 minutes

---

## 1. What This System Does

When a customer submits a maintenance request online, the system automatically:

- Sends them a confirmation email
- Collects their payment (if required)
- Sends them any forms they need to sign
- Adds the job to your calendar and tracking sheet

**You do not need to send anything manually.** The system handles all of that for you.

---

## 2. What Managers Need to Do

Your job is simple:

1. **Check the calendar or tracking sheet** to see new requests (see Section 5 below).
2. **Show up and complete the maintenance work** at the scheduled time.
3. **Mark the job as done** in the tracking sheet when finished.

That's it. No forms to send, no payment links to track down, no back-and-forth with customers over email.

---

## 3. What Customers Do

Customers go to the booking page on your website and fill out a short form. They enter:

- Their name and contact info
- Their unit number
- What kind of maintenance they need
- A preferred date and time

After they submit, they receive an automatic confirmation and any next steps — all without you lifting a finger.

---

## 4. What Happens for Lock-Cut Requests

A lock cut is a special request that needs a little more care. Here is what happens automatically:

1. The customer submits the request online.
2. The system collects their payment right away.
3. The system sends them an authorization form to sign digitally.
4. Once signed, the job shows up on your calendar and tracking sheet.

Again — you do not send the payment link or the form. The system does it. You just show up when the job is ready.

---

## 5. Where Managers Check Requests

All requests are tracked in two places:

- **Google Calendar** – Shows upcoming jobs by date and time so you can plan your day.
- **Google Sheets** – A running log of all requests, including status, unit number, and customer details.

Both update automatically when a new request comes in. You can check them from any phone or computer.

> **Tip:** Bookmark the Google Sheet so you can open it quickly each morning.

---

---

## 7. What to Do If Something Looks Wrong

If you notice any of the following, let your supervisor or the person who manages this system know:

| Situation | What to Do |
|---|---|
| A customer says they never got a confirmation email | Let your supervisor know — do not try to send it manually |
| A job appears on the calendar but looks incomplete | Check the tracking sheet for notes; flag it if unclear |
| A customer says they were charged but nothing is scheduled | Escalate to your supervisor right away |
| You don't see a job you were expecting | Check the tracking sheet first; if it's missing, flag it |

**When in doubt, flag it — don't guess.** The system keeps records of everything, so issues can usually be resolved quickly.
